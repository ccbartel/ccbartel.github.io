Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026.

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit Bartel Games (the "Site") and play its games.

Information we collect

The games run entirely in your browser. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, e-mail or other personal details while you play. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and operation of the service.

Some game features keep data on your own device (browser local storage): your best score, whether you played today's daily challenge, your language and the nickname you chose for the leaderboard.

Play counts, likes and leaderboards

To count plays, likes and leaderboard scores we use Supabase (a database service). Your browser creates a random anonymous ID that stays on your device; it is not linked to your name or e-mail. When you like a game or submit a score, we store that random ID, the game, the score, the date and the nickname you typed (if any). Nicknames are public on the leaderboard, so do not use your real full name. Play counts are stored only as a total per game. You can clear the anonymous ID by clearing this site's data in your browser; to remove a nickname or score already published, contact us at the address below.

Cookies and advertising

The Site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Site or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings (My Ad Center).
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info or www.youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Brazil under the LGPD), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; ads may still appear, but they will be less relevant.

Fonts and libraries

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and game libraries from the jsDelivr CDN. These services may receive your IP address as part of delivering the files.

Children

The Site is a general-audience site. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
