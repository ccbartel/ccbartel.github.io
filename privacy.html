Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026.

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit Bartel Games (the "Site") and play its games.

Information we collect

The games run entirely in your browser and you can always play without an account. Signing in with Google is optional and only keeps your nickname and best score on any device. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and operation of the service. To count visits to the site we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies and does not identify visitors: it only counts visits in aggregate (page, country, device type and where the visit came from). See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Some game features keep data on your own device (browser local storage): your best score, whether you played the daily challenge, your language and, if you signed in, your login session.

Play counts, likes and leaderboards

To count plays, likes and leaderboard scores we use Supabase (a database service). Plays are counted in a total per game and, to improve the games, each play is also recorded anonymously (duration, score and how it ended); likes use a random anonymous ID kept on your device. To appear in the leaderboard you only need to choose a nickname; it is linked to this device by a random ID and a secret code kept in your browser (on the server we keep only an irreversible digest of the code). Signing in with Google is optional and keeps your nickname and best score on any device: we then receive your name, e-mail address and profile photo from Google, but they are never shown in the leaderboard. We store your nickname, the device or account ID, the game, your best score and the date. The leaderboard shows only your nickname (unique, without offensive words, changeable once every 24 hours). To prevent abuse, when a nickname is saved we keep an irreversible digest of the IP address for up to 1 day. You can sign out at any time with the "Sign out" button in the leaderboard; to delete your account and your scores, write to us at the address below and we will remove them. We also measure anonymous game usage to improve the games: when a game is opened, started and finished, its duration, score and how it ended, sharing and likes, together with the random device ID, the language, whether it is a phone or a computer and the website you came from (domain only). This never includes your name, e-mail or account. You can reset this random ID by clearing this site's data in your browser. Anonymous events are kept for 13 months and then deleted automatically. Your IP address may briefly appear in the server's technical logs (Supabase/GitHub Pages), but it is not stored in the game data (only the 1-day irreversible digest mentioned above, against abuse).

The online mode of Bumpets runs on our game server at Cloudflare (Workers): while you are in a room it receives your nickname, a random ID of your device for this game and your moves, and shares your nickname and your animal with the other players in the room. Rooms are deleted when everyone leaves. To confirm your nickname, the server checks it with Supabase. Nothing else is stored.

The live race mode (Candy Line) uses Supabase Realtime: while you are in a room, your nickname, your progress in the race and whether you are connected are shared with the other players in that room. The room (code, nickname, progress and times) is deleted from the server within 3 hours of the last activity.

Community wall: on each game page you can post ideas, feedback and invitations to play, and reply to other players. A post shows your nickname, the text, the date and, if you add it, a room code. We store the post with your device or account ID (never shown) so you can delete your own posts and to limit spam. Posts with offensive words or links are refused, and a post reported by 3 players is hidden automatically; we may also remove posts that break the rules. Do not write personal information in posts. To delete a post, use its "Delete" button; to remove all your posts, write to us at the address below.

Fonts and libraries

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and game libraries from the jsDelivr CDN. These services may receive your IP address as part of delivering the files.

Children

The Site is a general-audience site. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
