Game guide

Crazy Parking: how to play, tips and FAQ

Crazy Parking is a one-tap 3D arcade parking game by Bartel Games. Your car drives on its own toward a parking spot, and you have exactly one action: pull the handbrake. Tap at the right moment and the car drifts, spins and slides sideways or backwards into the spot. Tap too early or too late and it stops short, overshoots, crashes into a parked car or flies into the water.

Every level is a different maneuver. In one you spin 270 degrees and reverse into a side spot, in another you jump over school buses, loop the loop, land on a trampoline or race a train across the tracks. The closer the car stops to the red spot, the more points you score, and a perfect stop is worth 1000.

How to play

  1. Choose Arcade, the Daily challenge or a level in Practice.
  2. The car starts moving by itself. Watch where the red spot and the red arrow are.
  3. Tap the screen once (or press Space or Enter) to pull the handbrake. The car drifts, spins and slides.
  4. Wait for it to stop. The score shows how close you got: right on the spot is a PERFECT.
  5. Tap again to go to the next level or try again.

Controls

  • Phone: tap anywhere on the game screen.
  • Computer: click the game, or press Space or Enter.
  • You only tap once per attempt. If you tap while the car is in the air, it starts spinning when the wheels touch the ground.

Game modes

  • Arcade: all 12 levels in a row with one try each. Your total goes to the global ranking, and at the end you can send a challenge link to a friend so they try to beat your score on the same levels.
  • Daily challenge: one goal for everyone today, such as getting 90% on a certain level, a PERFECT, or finding a secret spot. Unlimited tries, and you can share how many tries it took.
  • Practice: pick any unlocked level and repeat it as many times as you like. Here the score is a percentage, and 40% or more unlocks the next level.

Scoring

Each level is worth 0 to 1000 points depending on how close the car stops to the red spot. Crashing into a car or a wall, falling into the water, getting hit by the train or not pulling the handbrake at all gives 0. In Arcade the points of the 12 levels are added up, so one bad level does not end the run, but it costs you.

The 12 levels

The levels go from the widest timing window to the tightest: the parking lot, the gas station, the broken bridge, the stacked containers, the drawbridge, the snowy street, the bus jump, the shipping container, the railway crossing, the trampoline, the loop and, last, the cargo ship. Each one has its own camera and its own maneuver, so learning one does not solve the next.

Secret LEGENDARY spots

Some levels hide a secret place to stop. Find one and the screen shows 99999999 and LEGENDARY. In Arcade a legendary stop is worth 1500 points instead of 1000, the only way to go past a perfect run. We will not say exactly where they are, but one involves the roof of a bus and another a little boat near the ship.

Tips

  • The first times you play a level, a hand appears close to the right moment to tap. Use it to learn the rhythm.
  • If the result says TAPPED TOO EARLY or TAPPED TOO LATE, move your tap a little in that direction. The difference between 60% and 100% is often a tenth of a second.
  • On ramps, tap before the jump: the car spins in the air or right after landing.
  • On snow the car slides much further, so tap earlier than you think.
  • In Arcade, play safe on the hardest levels: a decent score beats a crash.

Frequently asked questions

Is Crazy Parking free?

Yes. All levels, the ranking, the daily challenge and friend challenges are free. The page shows ads.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It runs in the browser on phones and computers. No download and no sign-up.

How do I get into the ranking?

Finish Arcade and choose a nickname. Signing in with Google is optional and only keeps your nickname and record on other devices.

Why did I get 0 points?

The car crashed, fell into the water, was hit by the train or you did not tap. The result screen tells you which one, and whether you tapped too early or too late.

How do I challenge a friend?

At the end of Arcade tap Challenge a friend. They open the link, see your score and play the same 12 levels to beat it.
