Candy Shot

How to play

  1. Drag your finger (or mouse) left and right to aim, and let go to shoot the candy.
  2. Join 3 or more candies of the same color to pop them, before the line reaches the end of the track.
  3. Swipe up (right click or Space on a computer) to swap the candy with the next one. When both sides of a gap have the same color, they pull together: COMBO! Combos and finishing early give more points and up to 3 stars.

Game modes

  • Levels: 60 levels in 4 chapters, with bombs, ice, reverse and rainbow powers.
  • Endless: the line never stops and gets faster; make your best score and enter the ranking.
  • Daily: a new level every day, the same for everyone. Clear it and keep your streak of days.
  • Coop: two pudding shooters on the same track. Play with the computer, invite a friend with a link, or play with someone online.

Candy Shot: how to play, tips and FAQ

Candy Shot is a candy shooter made by Bartel Games. A line of candies rolls along a chocolate track toward a hole, and you control a little pudding at the bottom of the screen that shoots candies up at it.

Hit a candy of the same color to make a group of 3 or more and the group pops. Clear the whole line before the first candy reaches the hole and you win the level. There are 60 levels in 4 chapters, plus an endless mode, a daily level and co-op.

How to play

  1. Look at the candy on top of the pudding's head: that is the one you will shoot.
  2. Move the pudding sideways until it is under a candy of the same color on the track.
  3. Release to shoot straight up. The candy joins the line where it hits.
  4. Make a group of 3 or more of the same color and it pops. Bigger groups give more points.
  5. If the line reaches the hole at the end of the track, the level is lost and you can try again right away.

Controls

  • Phone: put your finger anywhere and slide it sideways. The pudding moves as much as your finger moves. Lift the finger to shoot.
  • Swap candies on the phone: swipe up quickly. The candy on the head trades places with the one in the pudding's belly.
  • Computer: the pudding follows the mouse, and a click shoots. Right-click or the space bar swaps the candies.

Points, combos and stars

Every candy in a popped group is worth 10 points. When a group pops in the middle of the line, the gap closes. If the two candies that touch are the same color and form a new group, it pops too: that is a COMBO, and each step of the chain multiplies the points (x2, x3 and so on).

Winning a level gives 1 star. The points bar at the top shows the marks for 2 and 3 stars. Clearing the line also sends a spark along the rest of the track that adds bonus points, so finishing early pays off. From the win screen you can replay the level to chase the missing stars.

Powers and special candies

  • Pop groups with 3 shots in a row and a power bubble appears where the last group burst. Shoot the bubble to give the power to the pudding; your next shot uses it.
  • Bomb: blows up the candies around where it hits.
  • Ice: freezes the line for 5 seconds.
  • Reverse: pushes the line backward, away from the hole.
  • Rainbow: takes the color of the candy it hits, so it always makes a match.
  • Some candies in the line have a ring and a small badge. A gold one gives +100 points when it pops; the others set off their power for free.
  • From level 10, grey stones appear in the line. They cannot be matched, but they break when a group next to them pops.

Game modes

  • Levels: 60 levels on a map. The first one uses only 3 colors; later chapters add more colors, faster lines and longer tracks.
  • Daily level: opens at level 4. A new level every day, the same for everyone.
  • Endless: opens at level 6. The line never ends and keeps getting faster and more colorful. Your best score goes to the global ranking.
  • Co-op: two puddings on the same track. Play with the computer, invite a friend with a link or code, or let the game find someone online. If nobody shows up in a few seconds, the computer joins you.

Tips and strategies

  • Aim for the spot where a pop will make two candies of the same color meet. One good shot can set off a whole chain.
  • When the candy on the head has no match on the screen, swap it instead of wasting the shot.
  • Keep the line far from the hole first and hunt for combos when it is safe.
  • Save the reverse and ice powers for when the front of the line gets close to the hole.
  • When the line gets close to the hole, it slows down a little. Use that moment to aim carefully instead of shooting in a hurry.

Frequently asked questions

Is Candy Shot free?

Yes. It is free to play, with ads on the page.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It runs in the browser on phones and computers, with no download and no sign-up.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, in this browser on this device. Clearing the browser data erases it.

How do I swap the candy?

On the phone, swipe up quickly. On the computer, right-click or press the space bar.

How do I get 3 stars?

Win the level with enough points to pass the third mark on the points bar. Combos and finishing early help the most.

Can I play with a friend?

Yes. In Co-op, tap Invite friend and send the link or code. You both shoot at the same line.
