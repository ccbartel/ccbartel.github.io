Candy Line

How to play

  1. The top bar shows a line of 3 candies.
  2. Find the same line in the grid - straight across or straight down, either way - and drag your finger (or mouse) over it.
  3. Every line you find fills a candy bag (1 bag = 1 point). Find it before the hint appears and you get extra time: +2 s, and in a row it's a COMBO: COMBO 2 = +2.5 s, COMBO 3 = +3 s, COMBO 4 or more = +3.5 s. A wrong line costs 2 seconds and ends the combo, and so does waiting for the hint. You start with 45 seconds: fill as many bags as you can!

Game modes

  • Play: new boards every time.
  • Daily mission: a new goal every day, the same for everyone (e.g. fill 6 bags before the time runs out). Unlimited tries!
  • Live race: 2 to 4 players race on the same boards, live. Create a race and send the code or link, join with a code, or play with someone random. The creator picks the goal (4 to 99 bags); first to get there wins. You need a nickname.
  • Challenge a friend: after a game, tap Challenge to send a link. Your friend plays exactly the same boards and tries to beat your number (challenge games don't count for the ranking).
  • Candy album: earn stickers for combos, full games, daily missions and more.

Candy Line is a free candy word-search puzzle: spot the row of sweets and swipe it before the time runs out. Plays in your browser on phone or computer. No download, no sign-up.
