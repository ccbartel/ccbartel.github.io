About Bartel Games

Bartel Games is a small independent game studio from Brazil. We design, program and test every game on this site ourselves: nothing here is copied from other portals or embedded from third parties. The same team also makes interactive game filters for TikTok, followed by more than 1.9 million people at @bartel_games.

Our idea is simple: short, polished games that open in a couple of seconds in any browser, on a phone or a computer, with no download and no account. You should be able to play a full round while waiting for the bus, and come back the next day for something new.

What you will find here

  • Quiz and puzzle games such as Geo Quiz, a geography quiz on a 3D globe with 76 countries, and Candy Line, a fast candy puzzle that can also be played live against friends.
  • Car games such as Burnout City, Drift Trail and The Last Driver, 3D driving games designed for one finger on a phone and the arrow keys on a computer.
  • Online party games with the Bumpets, cute animals inside bubbles: Arena, Soccer, Race, Zone, Flag, Paint and the card game Bumpets Cards. Up to 4 players join by link, and the computer fills empty seats.
  • A guide for every game right below it, with the rules, the modes, the controls, strategy tips and answers to common questions.

How we make the games

Every game starts as a tiny prototype that we play on real phones, including slow ones. We only publish when a new player can understand it in a few seconds without reading anything, when a round is short and when it runs smoothly on a modest phone. After launch we watch anonymous play data (for example, where people give up) and keep improving the games almost every day.

Art, 3D models and sound are made or adapted by us for each game, and the games are translated into several languages: English, Portuguese and Spanish, with French, German and Italian inside the games.

Our principles

  • Free to play. The site is funded by advertising; no game asks for payment.
  • No account needed. To appear in a ranking you only pick a nickname. Signing in with Google is optional.
  • Respect for privacy. Our own statistics are anonymous and cookie-free. Details are in the privacy policy.
  • Family-friendly content. No blood or graphic violence; at most cartoon action.
