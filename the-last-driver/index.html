The Last Driver

How to play

  1. Your car drives on its own down a zombie-filled road. Drag left or right (or use the arrow keys) to change lanes.
  2. Dodge cars, holes and lava. Press the jump button (S key) to leap over obstacles and the shoot button (A key) to blast what is in front of you. Jumps and shots are limited: grab pickups on the road to refill them.
  3. Run over zombies and collect coins. Hits take away life; the run ends when your life runs out or you fall into a hole, the water or the lava.
  4. Spend your coins in the Garage: new cars, upgrades, weapons and specials.

Missions: complete the three missions to level up and earn a coin bonus. Ranking: the score of each run counts for the global ranking.

The Last Driver is a free 3D zombie driving game: survive the road, smash zombies and upgrade your car. Plays in your browser on phone or computer. No download, no sign-up.
