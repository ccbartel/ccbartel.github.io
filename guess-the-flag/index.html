Guess the Flag

How to play

  1. The globe spins and drops a pin on a country.
  2. Tap the flag of that country. There is no timer: take your time.
  3. 3 rounds: a famous country, a medium one and a hard one. Try to get 3/3!

Guess the Flag is a free geography quiz with a realistic 3D globe. Learn the flags and locations of 46 countries, from the United States to Namibia, right in your browser on phone or computer. No download, no sign-up.
