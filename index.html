About Bartel Games

Bartel Games is a small independent studio from Brazil that makes free, original games for the browser. Every game here was designed, programmed and tested by us on real phones, so it opens in seconds and plays smoothly on a phone or a computer, with no download and no account.

You will find a geography quiz on a 3D globe, a fast candy puzzle, 3D car games and online party games with the Bumpets that you can play with up to 3 friends just by sending a link. Below every game there is a full guide with the rules, the controls, strategy tips and answers to common questions.

New games and updates arrive almost every day. Read more about us or send us your idea.
